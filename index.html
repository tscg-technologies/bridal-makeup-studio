Step-by-Step Journey

THE BRIDAL EXPERIENCE

From the first enquiry to your wedding day entrance, experience a calm, professional, and personalized styling process.

01

Initial Consultation

We discuss your wedding celebration date, venues, outfit color schemes, jewellery themes, and personal makeup preferences.

02

Bridal Look Planning

Detailed styling notes are curated for your ceremony times, lighting, dupatta or saree draping style, and skin preparation regimen.

03

Makeup Trial

An optional pre-wedding trial session (subject to booking package) to test foundation textures, eye definitions, and bridal comfort.

04

Wedding Day Makeup

Punctual, organized, and tranquil on-location or in-studio application, ensuring you are calm, radiant, and ready on schedule.
