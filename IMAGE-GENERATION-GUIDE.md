## 1. Overview & Visual Aesthetic Guidelines

This document provides a complete inventory and copy-ready generation prompts for all image assets used across the Bridal Makeup Studio website. All images are to be generated using **Google Flow** and subsequently saved into the `assets/images/` directory with their exact filenames.

### Strict Visual Guidelines for Google Flow:
* **Photography Style:** High-end bridal beauty editorial, Vogue India / Harper's Bazaar Bride aesthetic.
* **Skin & Texture:** Realistic, natural skin pores and luminous skin texture. Strictly avoid rubbery, plastic, or overly AI-smoothed finishes.
* **Lighting:** Professional studio softbox lighting with warm golden keylights and subtle hair rim lighting.
* **Diversity & Variety:** Varied Indian skin undertones (warm golden, deep olive, honey bronze, fair porcelain, wheatish), diverse facial features, expressions, and age profiles across services.
* **Wardrobe & Jewellery:** Authentic Indian bridal handloom silks (Kanjeevaram, Banarasi), intricate zardozi/gotapatti lehengas, authentic heritage temple jewellery, uncut diamonds (polki), kundan, and emerald beads.
* **Negative Space:** Clean, editorial compositions with negative space where banner overlays or text might appear.
* **Prohibitions:** Strictly NO logos, NO brand names, NO watermarks, NO AI-generated text, NO distorted hands or jewellery.

   ```
   assets/
   └── images/
       ├── hero-bridal-makeup.webp
       ├── about-makeup-artist.webp
       └── ... (all 44 webp files)
   ```

3. **Automatic or Instant Display:**  
   The website's placeholder system is designed to seamlessly accept images. To display an image in place of the CSS placeholder, you can simply insert an `<img>` tag inside the placeholder container or replace the placeholder container with:
   ```html
   <img src="assets/images/hero-bridal-makeup.webp" alt="Timeless Indian Bride Portrait" class="aspect-4-5" style="object-fit: cover; width: 100%; border-radius: var(--radius-sm);">
   ```
   Or leave the placeholder container intact and add the `<img>` element inside it; the CSS handles `object-fit: cover` and proper scaling automatically.
