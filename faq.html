FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about booking timelines, pre-wedding trials, styling inclusions, and venue travel.

How early should I book my bridal makeup?

+

It is generally recommended to enquire 3 to 6 months in advance, especially during the peak Indian wedding seasons (such as October to February and May to July). Because auspicious wedding dates (muhurtham days) book quickly, reaching out early ensures our team can reserve your slot exclusively.

Is a bridal makeup trial available?

+

Yes, bridal makeup trials can be arranged subject to studio schedule availability and the selected booking package. A trial allows you to discuss undertones, sample eyeliner and foundation textures, test comfort, and preview how the look photographs under studio lighting.

What is included in a bridal makeup package?

+

Depending on whether you choose the Essential, Signature, or Luxury package, inclusions may feature custom skin preparation, HD or airbrush makeup application, false lashes, bridal hairstyling, saree or dupatta draping, and jewellery placement assistance. Exact package details are customized during consultation.

Do you provide hairstyling?

+

Yes, bridal hairstyling is offered as part of our integrated bridal styling services (if offered by the studio). Options range from traditional South Indian braided styles with jasmine flowers and billai to modern textured updos, Hollywood waves, and messy buns.

Is saree or dupatta draping available?

+

Yes, professional draping assistance can be included for silk Kanjeevaram sarees, bridal lehenga double-dupattas, and contemporary gowns. We ensure pleats are crisp, securely pinned, and comfortable for walking and sitting through rituals.

Do you provide makeup for bridesmaids and family members?

+

Yes, party and guest makeup is available for bridesmaids, mothers, and close family members. For groups larger than 3 people, additional senior assistants may be scheduled to ensure everyone is ready on time without rushing the bride.

Do you travel to wedding venues and hotel rooms?

+

Yes, our artists are available for on-location appointments at banquet halls, resort venues, and hotel dressing suites. We arrive equipped with daylight vanity lamps, sanitized kits, and styling tools for a seamless on-site experience.

Are travel charges included in the package?

+

Studio policy to be added. Travel fees, outstation accommodation, and local transport depend on distance from the studio base in Hyderabad to the event venue, and are detailed transparently in your formal quote.

How is the booking advance determined?

+

Studio policy to be added. Typically, a nominal percentage advance is required to formally block and guarantee the artist's availability for your wedding date on the studio calendar.

Can I reschedule my booking if wedding dates change?

+

Studio policy to be added. Rescheduling is accommodating wherever feasible, subject to calendar availability for your revised event date. Early written notice is recommended.

How many people can be accommodated in one event?

+

We accommodate intimate single-bride bookings as well as bridal parties of 6 to 12 people. When multiple attendees require styling, a team of dedicated artists and hairstylists is coordinated based on your event timeline.

Which makeup styles are available?

+

We specialize in Traditional South Indian muhurtham looks, North Indian royal lehenga glam, HD natural camera finishes, Airbrush weightless applications, Minimalist dewy aesthetics, and dramatic Evening Reception looks.
